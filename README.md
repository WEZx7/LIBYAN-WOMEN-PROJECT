# Libyan Women Project

**A digital initiative supporting the safety, rights, and voices of women and girls in Libya.**

[![Live Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-Visit%20Project-8A5A8A?style=for-the-badge)](https://libyanwomenproject.ly/)

## About

Libyan Women Project is an independent initiative that creates space for women and girls in Libya to learn, share experiences, and raise awareness about issues that affect their rights and wellbeing.

The project focuses on listening to women's experiences without judgment, documenting broader patterns without exposing personal identities, and turning those experiences into greater public awareness.

## What the Website Offers

- **Awareness and learning:** Resources related to women's rights, education, digital safety, mental and physical health, and violence prevention.
- **Stories and experiences:** A space that helps bring attention to the experiences and concerns of women and girls.
- **Safe reporting:** An option to report an incident without being asked for information that reveals the reporter's identity.
- **Contact:** A way to reach the project team with questions and inquiries.

## Mission

To help make information and support-related resources more accessible, encourage safer conversations, and amplify the voices of women and girls in Libya.

## Guiding Principles

- **Respect:** Listen without judgment.
- **Privacy:** Avoid collecting identifying information through the project's public-facing experience.
- **Awareness:** Share educational information and highlight issues that deserve attention.
- **Dignity:** Treat every experience with care and respect.
